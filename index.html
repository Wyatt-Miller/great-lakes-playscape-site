Climb & Explore

Climb to new heights with the kid-sized climbing wall and treehouse. Our facility does have crash mats and padded flooring throughout our gross motor play areas.

There's a fire truck, a playhouse, a dress-up corner, and so much more. Kids pretty much come up with the rest on their own.

Secure & Convenient Access

Members and day passers who have completed a waiver and payment may enter our space via the GymMaster app. Our facility is equipped with high-end cameras to ensure your safety. Parent supervision is required at all times.
