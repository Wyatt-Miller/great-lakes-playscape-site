Frequently Asked Questions

Don't see your question here? Email hello@greatlakesplayscape.com.

When do you open?

We're opening by January 1, 2027. Once we're open, hours are 8 AM to 8 PM every day.

How do I get in?

Sign up online, sign the waiver, and pay for a membership or pass. Then you unlock the front door with the GymMaster app.

Is the space secure?

Yes. The front door stays locked, and only people who have signed our waiver and paid for a membership or pass can unlock it with the GymMaster app. Door access is just for your household, and the space has high-end security cameras. Every kid also has to be with a parent or guardian the whole time, and one adult can bring up to four kids.

Do I have to stay with my kids?

Yes. A parent or guardian has to supervise their kids the whole time.

Do babies need a pass?

Infants under 1 play free with a paid sibling.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, anytime. Cancel in the GymMaster app or member portal, or email us. It ends at the end of your current billing period.

Do punch cards expire?

No. Punch card visits never expire.

Does the price include tax?

Yes. All prices on the site include sales tax.

How do I book a birthday party?

Email hello@greatlakesplayscape.com to check dates and book. See the party packages for what's included.
