What's inside

Room to run around, even in January

Winters up here are long. This gives kids a warm place to burn off some energy and play with other kids, any time of year.

Climb & Explore

A kid-sized climbing wall with rings, a cargo net, and a ladder, with crash mats underneath. There are soft foam blocks for the littler ones too.

Play & Imagine

There's a fire truck, a playhouse, and a slide. Kids pretty much come up with the rest on their own.

Comfy for Grown-ups

There's a comfy spot for parents too, with coffee and snacks close by. Sit down, talk to the other parents, and let the kids play.

Members Come & Go

Members unlock the front door with an app, so you can come in whenever it works for you, around naps or whatever else your day looks like.

Who we are

We're a local family

We're Wyatt and Claire Miller, and Great Lakes Playscape is our business. Between the two of us we've worked in education, child development, youth programs, and running a business. If you've got kids around here, you already know the problem: winter lasts forever and there aren't many indoor places where kids can really run around. So we decided to build one.
