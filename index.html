An indoor playground for little Twin Ports adventurers.

Great Lakes Playscape is a bright, friendly indoor play space where kids climb, tumble, and imagine — rain, snow, or shine. Built by a local family, for local families.

What's inside

Room to run when the weather won't cooperate

Northern Minnesota winters are long. Great Lakes Playscape gives kids a warm, safe place to burn energy and make friends all year round.

Climb & Explore

Soft play structures, a kid-sized climbing wall, and open room to move — designed for little ones to challenge themselves safely.

Play & Imagine

Themed play areas, including a fire truck and a playhouse, that spark pretend, creativity, and the kind of unstructured fun kids remember.

Comfy for Grown-ups

A relaxed spot for parents with coffee and snacks close by, so you can watch, chat, and actually take a breath.

Members Come & Go

Membership includes app-based door access, so visits fit around nap times and busy days — on your schedule.

Who we are

A family business, built for local families

Great Lakes Playscape is owned and run by Wyatt & Claire Miller, a husband-and-wife team with backgrounds in education, child development, youth programming, and business operations. We started this because we saw the same thing so many Twin Ports parents already know — long winters mean too few indoor places for kids to really run and play. So we're building the one we wished we had.
